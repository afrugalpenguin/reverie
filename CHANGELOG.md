**v1.0.0**

- Welcome tour: on your first login, a short hands-on tour shows the overlay, takes a test photo, lets you pick a photo key and points you to the options. Replay it any time with /reverie tour or from Options › AddOns › Reverie. If you already use Reverie, you get a one-line chat note instead.
- Photos taken while the overlay is up keep its cinema bars and title card, at full strength even if the text had faded. The chat box is still left out.
- The overlay's chat box now sits where your game chat window is.
- Photo stamp: new Level and Race, class tickboxes ("Level 42 Night Elf Hunter"). Level-up photos show your new level.
- The photo key button asks before taking a key that already does something (such as / for chat): press it again to confirm.
- Fixed a Lua error ("secret boolean value") that could repeat while the game was restricting what addons can read.

**v0.3.1**

- The "Take photo" key binding now shows up in Options › Keybindings › Reverie.
- Set the photo key from a button at the bottom of the Photo tab, or under Options › AddOns › Reverie without opening the window: click it and press a key, Escape cancels, right-click clears.

**v0.3.0**

- "Show chat" option (on by default): while the overlay is up, a faded chat box at the bottom right shows guild, officer, whisper, party and raid messages, so you don't miss them on a long flight. Each message fades after 20 seconds.
- The chat box is left out of photos and automatic screenshots.

**v0.2.0**

- Photo mode: /reverie photo or the "Take photo" key binding hides the interface, shows framing guides and a self-timer, then saves one screenshot for each shot type ticked on the new Photo tab (clean, stamped, vignetted, saturated, sepia), with optional portrait, profile or wide camera presets. Escape cancels, and everything is put back afterwards, even after a crash or reload.
- Automatic photos on level up, first visit to a zone, achievements and boss kills: a normal screenshot straight away, then a clean photo. In combat the photo waits until combat ends (up to 30 seconds).
- Photos hide enemy nameplates, names and other addons' waypoint arrows, and are never taken in combat.
- /reverie unlock lets you drag text elements anywhere; /reverie lock or Escape finishes.
- /reverie text commands show, hide, pin or free each text element.
- Faded text comes back when you move the mouse.
- The options window now has Text and Photo tabs.
- Settings from 0.1.x carry over automatically.

**v0.1.1**

- The camera orbit is about a third slower.

**v0.1.0**

- First release, for WoW Forever.
- Letterbox bars and a title card (name, guild, realm, level/race/class, zone, subzone, away timer) on flight paths, flying mounts and while AFK.
- Hides the interface while the overlay is up, with an optional slow camera orbit and zoom out.
- Text fades after 10 seconds, Escape dismisses the overlay, and it never starts in combat and closes if combat begins.
- Options window (/reverie) with a live preview: show or hide each text element, place it on a 3x3 grid or freely, and toggle triggers, bars, fade and camera. Also listed in Settings > AddOns.
- "Show surname" option for Forever's two-part character names.
- Your own name above your character is hidden while the overlay is up, and restored afterwards.
